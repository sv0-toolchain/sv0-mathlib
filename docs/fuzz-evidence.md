## Invariants

- **FZ-TRIG-IDENTITY-001**: sin(x)^2 + cos(x)^2 within 1e-7 of 1 over [-1000*pi, 1000*pi] (oracle: the identity itself (no separate reference))
- **FZ-POLAR-ROUNDTRIP-001**: from_polar(to_polar(x, y)) recovers (x, y) within 1e-4 for points up to 1e6, including near-origin (oracle: the identity itself (no separate reference))
- **FZ-MOD-INVERSE-001**: for coprime (a, m), mul_mod(a, mod_inverse(a, m), m) == 1; at least 15 coprime draws actually checked (oracle: the identity itself (no separate reference))
- **FZ-COMPLEX-ALGEBRA-001**: complex addition and multiplication are commutative and associative within tolerance (oracle: the identities themselves (no separate reference))
- **FZ-STATS-001**: min <= mean <= max; variance >= 0; sample >= population variance; sum == mean*n; variance shift-invariant and x9 under x3; mean shifts and scales (oracle: the identities themselves (no separate reference))

## Adding or changing a property

1. Put the property in its own `test/property/<name>_property.sv0` with
   `let ROUNDS: i32 = N;` and `let mut state: u64 = SEED;` (a seed no other fixture uses)
   and a `while ... < ROUNDS` loop.
2. Add a row to `docs/fuzz.tsv` with the same seed and rounds and a floor.
3. Run `python3 scripts/check_fuzz_budget.py --write-md`.

To raise the budget, change `ROUNDS`, `rounds` and (to ratchet) `min_rounds`
together. Lowering `min_rounds` is a deliberate, reviewable edit to this manifest.
